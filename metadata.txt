about=When working with QGIS Processing tools, results are often stored as temporary (in-memory) layers that disappear when you close the project. This plugin detects temporary layers and saves selected vector and raster layers into one GeoPackage, as separate feature or raster tables. No external dependencies required — works on Windows, Linux, and macOS with a standard QGIS 3.x installation.
